Formnext 2026, as it happens — the five-axis wire

A dated log of what is actually announced at Formnext 2026 (17–20 November, Messe Frankfurt) that touches five-axis and non-planar printing — updated once a day while the show runs. The stands, deadlines and background are in the pre-show guide; this page deliberately does not repeat them.

How this wire works

An item appears here only if it has a direct five-axis, non-planar or multi-axis angle and can be sourced to the organiser or the exhibitor. Items announced in advance are marked scheduled and are updated once the announcement is public. If a watched reveal turns out to have no multi-axis angle, the wire says so and closes the item — it does not stretch a three-axis product into a five-axis story. Figures that cannot be verified from a primary source are not published.

Watching on day one — Tuesday 17 November

Two scheduled items share one booth on the opening day. Both are logged now, before the show, so the record shows what was promised in advance — and can be checked against what is actually announced.

Scheduled: Stratasys platform reveal — Tue 17 Nov, 13:00, Hall 12.1, Booth D129

Stratasys has announced that it will unveil a new manufacturing-scale solution at its booth on the opening day, Tuesday 17 November at 13:00. The name, the process and the specifications are being held back until the reveal itself — the company's own release says only that full details will be announced at Formnext.

Why a five-axis wire is watching a reveal it knows nothing about: this is the show's largest scheduled unknown, and the wire's job during the week is to check major platform announcements for multi-axis or non-planar content as they land. Nothing published so far describes the new platform as a five-axis system, and this entry claims no such thing. It will be updated after 13:00 on 17 November with what was actually announced — including if the answer is that there is no multi-axis angle.

Status: scheduled. Source: Stratasys press release, 29 October 2026 (see Sources).

F870 European debut — same booth, Hall 12.1, Booth D129

Sharing the booth and the moment is the European debut of the F870, Stratasys's large-format FDM system, first launched in September. Per the company, it is built for factory-floor production of tooling, fixtures, jigs and manufacturing aids, with a fully heated build chamber of 1,000 × 610 × 610 mm and extended unattended operation, so that manufacturers can bring large tooling work in-house. Factory-floor applications from Toyota and GM are part of the same exhibit.

An honest note, in the same spirit as the guide's adjacent flags: the F870 is a large-format Cartesian FDM machine, not a five-axis printer. It is logged here because large, curved tooling is precisely the class of work where non-planar deposition is proposed as the alternative — and because what the week's largest FDM vendor chooses to show frames the questions the five-axis exhibitors have to answer. If a five-axis answer to this class of work appears during the show, it will be briefed below.

Status: confirmed pre-show announcement. Source: Stratasys press release, 29 October 2026 (see Sources).

No briefs yet — the show opens on Tuesday 17 November. Sourced briefs will be added here once a day, newest first, through Friday 20 November. Exhibitors with a five-axis or non-planar announcement that should be on this wire can reach the desk via the contact page.

Frequently asked questions

How is this page different from the Formnext 2026 guide?+

The guide is the pre-show briefing: the stands, dates and deadlines known before the doors open. This wire is the during-show log: dated, sourced briefs on what is actually announced between 17 and 20 November. The two pages do not repeat each other.

What qualifies for this wire?+

Only announcements with a direct five-axis, non-planar or multi-axis angle, each backed by an organiser or exhibitor source. Scheduled reveals are marked as scheduled and updated once the announcement is public. Figures that cannot be verified from a primary source are not published.

How often is the wire updated?+

Once a day from Tuesday 17 to Friday 20 November 2026. After the show closes, the page stays as the record of the week and is no longer updated.
