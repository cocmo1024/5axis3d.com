Privacy Policy

How this site handles your data — briefly, because there is not much of it.

The short version

This is a static website. There are no user accounts, no comments, and no advertising trackers. We do not set cookies. The contact form does not submit anything to a server — it composes an email in your own mail client, and you choose whether to send it.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see aggregate traffic: which pages are read, roughly where readers are, and which sites refer them. It is a privacy-first service — no cookies, no visitor fingerprinting, no cross-site tracking. We see statistics, not individuals.

Email you send us

If you write to us, your message arrives as ordinary email. We use it only to reply and, if it leads somewhere, to discuss your project. We do not sell, rent or share email addresses, and we do not add you to a mailing list. Ask us to delete your messages and we will.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, whose servers may process standard request logs (IP address, user agent) to serve and protect the site, under Cloudflare's own privacy policy. Type is loaded from Google Fonts; fetching those files may expose your IP address to Google, as with any externally hosted font.

External links

We link to projects and resources elsewhere on the web. Their privacy practices are their own.

Changes

If this policy changes, the change will appear on this page with a new date below.
